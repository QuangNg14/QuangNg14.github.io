Teaching in public

Pathwise Mentorship ↗

From an internship search to a mentorship program.

I co-founded Pathwise Mentorship in December 2023 for students navigating careers in tech and finance, and US university admissions. We work through résumés, projects, interview practice, and the decisions that come after an offer. I also help administer Pathwise Network, a community of about 38,000 members as of November 2026.

Alongside one-to-one coaching, the technical curriculum has 32 lessons, progressing from complexity and arrays to graphs and dynamic programming.
